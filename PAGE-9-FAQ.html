Frequently Asked Questions | C Line Cars Airport Taxi

Have questions about our airport transfer service? Find answers to the most common questions about booking, pricing, policies, and our service.

How Do I Book an Airport Transfer?

Booking is simple! You have three options: (1) Fill out our online booking form on this website and we'll confirm within 2 hours, (2) Call us directly on 07808065494, or (3) Send us a WhatsApp message to the same number. We're available 24/7 so you can book at your convenience.

Are Your Prices Really Fixed?

Yes, absolutely. When we quote you a price, that's the price you'll pay. There are no meter charges, no surprise fuel surcharges, no congestion fees - just the agreed fixed price. The only additions are optional services like Meet & Greet (£5), Executive service (£10), or additional luggage (£20 per item).

What If My Flight Is Delayed?

That's exactly why we offer flight tracking. We monitor your flight in real-time using live airline data. If your flight is delayed, we automatically adjust your pickup time. You never pay extra for delays - your price remains fixed at the agreed amount.

What Payment Methods Do You Accept?

We accept all major payment methods including cash, credit cards, debit cards, and digital payments. You can pay at booking or in the vehicle - whatever suits you best.

Do You Operate Outside Standard Hours?

Yes! We operate 24/7, including early mornings and late nights. A small surcharge of £20 applies for journeys between 10 PM and 6 AM to cover unsociable hours, but we're always available when you need us.

Can I Bring Luggage and Baggage?

Of course! All our vehicles are designed to handle airport luggage. Standard suitcases and holiday baggage are included in your price. If you need extra space for additional items, we charge £20 per extra bag. We can usually accommodate most requirements - just let us know when booking.

Is Your Fleet Suitable for Families?

Absolutely. We have vehicles to suit all needs - comfortable saloons for couples and individuals, spacious estate cars for families with luggage, and minibuses for larger groups. Car seats for children are available at no extra cost - just request when booking.

How Far in Advance Should I Book?

While we recommend booking 24-48 hours in advance, we can often accommodate last-minute bookings. Call or WhatsApp us with your details and we'll do our best to arrange a driver. In emergencies, we're here to help.

Are Your Drivers Experienced With Airport Routes?

Yes, all our drivers have extensive experience with airport routes. They know the best routes to each major airport, understand traffic patterns, and can advise you on timings. Many have been with us for years, giving you the benefit of their experience.

What Makes C Line Cars Different?

Several things: our transparent fixed pricing means no hidden charges, our flight tracking removes uncertainty, our drivers are all fully licensed and professionally trained, we've been serving customers for 14+ years, and we maintain consistently high standards.

What If I Need to Cancel or Modify My Booking?

Simply contact us as soon as possible. If you cancel more than 24 hours before your scheduled transfer, there's no charge. If you cancel within 24 hours, a 50% cancellation charge applies. If you need to modify your booking, we'll do our best to accommodate changes.

Can You Help With Special Requirements?

Yes! Whether you need wheelchair accessibility, additional luggage space, child car seats, or have other special requests, let us know at booking. We'll do our best to accommodate your needs. Our professional team can usually help with most situations.

How Do I Know My Transfer Will Be on Time?

Punctuality is a cornerstone of our service. Our drivers allow extra time for traffic congestion, road works, and seasonal variations. We pick you up with sufficient time to get you to the airport with a comfortable margin. Journey times are professionally estimated based on our 14+ years of experience.

Is Flight Tracking Automatic or Do I Need to Book It?

Flight tracking is included in all our bookings at no extra cost. When you provide your flight number, we automatically monitor your flight status. If there are delays or changes, we adjust our driver schedule accordingly.
