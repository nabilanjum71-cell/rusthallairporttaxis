Route Information

Rusthall to Gatwick — Everything You Need to Know

Gatwick Airport is the closest major UK airport to Rusthall at approximately 29 miles via the A21 and A23. Our drivers have completed this route in every traffic condition across every hour of the day and night. Here is exactly what to expect.

🛣️ The Route: Rusthall → Gatwick

We depart Rusthall via Pembury Road or the A264, join the A21 southbound through Tonbridge, continue via the A21/A26 to the M25 Junction 6, then south on the M23 direct to Gatwick. Journey time is typically 45–55 minutes in normal conditions. For early morning pickups (before 6am), we achieve it regularly in 40 minutes.

🛬 North vs South Terminal

Tell us your terminal when you book and we drop you at the exact correct entrance. South Terminal serves EasyJet, British Airways, Vueling, and many others. North Terminal serves TUI, Norwegian, Wizz Air and easyJet on some routes. If you're unsure, check your airline's website — or just tell us your airline and we'll confirm for you.

🌙 Early Morning Gatwick Runs from Rusthall

The majority of Gatwick flights from Rusthall TN4 depart between 6am and 9am — meaning a pickup between 3am and 6am. This is our most requested slot. Our drivers are dedicated early-morning specialists. We have never cancelled or been late for a 3am or 4am Gatwick pickup in 13 years of operation.

📡 Flight Tracking — Automatic on Every Booking

When you book your Rusthall to Gatwick transfer, we immediately register your flight number in our tracking system. If your outbound flight is delayed or your inbound Gatwick arrival is pushed back, our driver's schedule adjusts automatically. You will receive an SMS update. You do not need to call us.

🧳 Luggage & Group Transfers

Standard holiday luggage (one case per passenger) is included in the £85 fare. Additional bags are £20 per extra item. For groups of 5–8 passengers or large amounts of luggage, we send an executive minivan — same fixed price, confirmed at booking. Child seats are free — just request when booking and tell us your child's age.

🔄 Return Journey: Gatwick → Rusthall

The return fare from Gatwick Airport back to Rusthall is also £85, fixed. We meet you in the arrivals hall (Meet & Greet +£5) or at the designated taxi meeting point. We monitor your inbound flight and wait up to 45 minutes for free if it is delayed. Book your return at the same time as your outbound and receive confirmed pricing for both legs.
