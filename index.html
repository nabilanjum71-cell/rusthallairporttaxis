Frequently Asked Questions About Our Rusthall Airport Taxi Service

We get a lot of questions about booking, pricing, and our service. Here are answers to the most common ones:

How do I book an airport taxi with C Line Cars?

Booking is easy! You have three options: (1) Fill out our online booking form on this website and we'll confirm within 2 hours, (2) Call us directly on 07808065494, or (3) Send us a WhatsApp message to the same number for quick response. We're available 24/7 so you can book at your convenience.

Are your prices really fixed with no hidden charges?

Yes, absolutely. When we quote you a price, that's the price you'll pay. There are no meter charges, no surprise fuel surcharges, no congestion fees - just the agreed fixed price. The only additions are optional services like Meet & Greet (£5), Executive service (£10), or additional luggage (£20 per item). Everything is clearly explained at booking.

What if my flight is delayed?

That's exactly why we offer flight tracking. We monitor your flight in real-time, and if it's delayed, we automatically adjust your pickup time. You can also contact us directly with any changes. We understand that flights don't always run on schedule, and we build this into our service.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods including cash, credit cards, debit cards, and digital payments. You can pay at booking or in the vehicle - whatever suits you best.

Do you operate outside standard airport hours?

Yes! We operate 24/7, including early mornings and late nights. A small surcharge of £20 applies for journeys between 10 PM and 6 AM to cover unsociable hours, but we're always available when you need us.

Can I bring luggage and baggage?

Of course! All our vehicles are designed to handle airport luggage. Standard suitcases and holiday baggage are included in your price. If you need extra space for additional items, we charge £20 per extra bag. We can usually accommodate most requirements - just let us know when booking.

Is your fleet suitable for families?

Absolutely. We have vehicles to suit all needs - comfortable saloons for couples and individuals, spacious estate cars for families with luggage, and minibuses for larger groups. Car seats for children are available at no extra cost - just request when booking.

How far in advance should I book?

While we recommend booking 24-48 hours in advance, we can often accommodate last-minute bookings. Call or WhatsApp us with your details and we'll do our best to arrange a driver. In emergencies, we're here to help.

Are your drivers experienced with airport routes?

Yes, all our drivers have extensive experience with airport routes. They know the best routes to each major airport, understand traffic patterns, and can advise you on timings. Many have been with us for years, giving you the benefit of their experience.

What makes C Line Cars different from other taxi services?
