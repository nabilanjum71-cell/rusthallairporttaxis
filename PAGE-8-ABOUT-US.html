About C Line Cars | Professional Airport Taxi Service

C Line Cars has been serving Kent and East Sussex with professional airport transfer services since 2012. With over 14 years of experience, 50+ drivers, and 18+ modern vehicles, we're committed to exceptional service.

Our Story: 14+ Years of Professional Service

C Line Cars was founded in 2012 with a simple mission: provide reliable, professional airport transfer services at fair, transparent prices. What started as a small operation has grown into a thriving business serving hundreds of customers monthly.

For over 14 years, we've built our reputation on consistency, reliability, and exceptional customer service. We understand that airport transfers are important - whether for holidays, business, or family visits - and we treat every journey with the professionalism it deserves.

Our Values

Reliability: We arrive on time, every time. Our drivers are trained to manage traffic, plan routes, and ensure punctual arrivals.

Transparency: Our fixed-price model eliminates hidden charges. You know your cost upfront.

Safety: All drivers are CRB checked, fully licensed, and professionally trained. Your safety is our priority.

Professionalism: Our drivers are courteous, knowledgeable, and focused on your comfort.

Quality: Our vehicles are modern, well-maintained, and provide comfortable journeys.

Our Fleet

C Line Cars operates 18+ modern vehicles suitable for all travel needs. Our fleet includes comfortable saloon cars for individuals and couples, spacious estate cars for families and luggage, and minibuses for larger groups. All vehicles are:

Air-conditioned and climate-controlled for comfort. Equipped with modern safety features and satellite navigation. Regularly serviced and maintained to high standards. Operated by licensed, professionally-trained drivers.

Our Team

We employ 50+ professional drivers, all fully licensed and CRB checked. Our drivers receive ongoing training in customer service, route optimization, and safety procedures. Many have been with us for years, bringing extensive experience and local knowledge.

Beyond drivers, our office team provides 24/7 support. Whether you need to book, modify reservations, or get help, we're available whenever you need us.
