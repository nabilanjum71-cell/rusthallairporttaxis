About Our Tunbridge Wells Service

Airport Taxis from Royal Tunbridge Wells — Full Guide

Royal Tunbridge Wells is one of the most affluent towns in Kent, with a significant number of residents who travel internationally for business and leisure. Getting from Tunbridge Wells to a London airport has historically been a choice between expensive long-stay parking, complex train journeys via London, or booking a local taxi that may or may not be reliable at 4am.

We exist to remove that uncertainty. Based in Rusthall, two miles from the town centre, we are a dedicated airport taxi service for Tunbridge Wells and the surrounding TN postcode area. We do not do school runs, night-out pickups or general town centre fares. Every driver, every vehicle and every process is built around one thing: getting Tunbridge Wells residents to their airport on time, stress-free, at a price agreed before they've even left the house.

Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick — Our Most Popular Route

Gatwick is the airport of choice for most Tunbridge Wells passengers. At roughly 25–30 miles — usually via the A264 through East Grinstead, or the A21, M25 and M23 when that is quicker — it is the closest major airport and serves the widest range of holiday and short-haul destinations. Our fixed fare from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick is £85 — the same price every time, regardless of the hour or day of the week.

Tunbridge Wells to Heathrow — Long-Haul Specialist

For long-haul flights to the USA, Middle East, Asia or Australia, Heathrow is the airport of choice. Our £126 fixed fare from Tunbridge Wells covers Terminals 2, 3, 4 and 5, via the A21 and M25 (roughly 55 miles). We recommend booking early for any Heathrow departure, particularly for flights during school holiday periods when our early-morning slots fill quickly.
