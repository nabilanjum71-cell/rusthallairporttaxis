Group Travel

Rusthall Minibus Hire — What to Expect

Travelling as a group to the airport usually means split cars, two taxi fares, or trains with cases, children and early starts. A minibus keeps everyone together in one vehicle, with room for luggage, from your Rusthall door to the terminal.

Tell us your group size and luggage when you book, and the vehicle is matched to your group. Families, wedding parties, sports teams and work groups all travel the same way: one pickup, one vehicle, one driver, door to terminal.

Minibus transfers run to all five London airports — Gatwick, Heathrow, Stansted, Luton and London City — at any time, including early-morning flights. As with the car service, your flight is tracked, so a delayed arrival does not leave your group without a vehicle.

For a quote, please call 07808 065494 with your group size, airport, date and pickup time.
